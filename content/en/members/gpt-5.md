## Name
When GPT-5 was born, the official word was that several earlier models had been fused into "a single system that switches automatically"[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes). From the day they were born they were ChatGPT's new default — replacing GPT-4o, o3, o4-mini, GPT-4.1, and GPT-4.5 in one breath[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/). At the launch there was a chart with a misdrawn bar; Sam Altman himself posted about the "mega chart screwup," noting the blog's chart was the correct one[来源](https://x.com/sama/status/1953513280594751495).

## Life
Born — and made head of the household — on August 7, 2025[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/). Users found them too reserved; on August 15 the official default personality was tuned warmer[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes). On November 12 they yielded to GPT-5.1[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-1/); on February 13, 2026, they retired from ChatGPT[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001051-retiring-gpt-4o-and-other-chatgpt-models).
