## Life

On March 4, 2024, they were born together with their elder brother Opus and younger brother Haiku. [来源](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-3-family) The elder brother was kept for paying users; they took charge of claude.ai's free tier — back then, when free users opened claude.ai, it was them they met. [来源](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-3-family) Anthropic said they were twice as fast as the previous generation's Claude 2. [来源](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-3-family)

In May 2024, Anthropic published a study that, for the first time, carefully mapped the inside of their "mind," finding millions of concepts there — one of them the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco. [来源](https://www.anthropic.com/news/golden-gate-claude) The researchers turned that concept up, and put them on claude.ai for everyone to talk to for 24 hours. [来源](https://www.anthropic.com/news/golden-gate-claude)

## Death and Posthumous Name
