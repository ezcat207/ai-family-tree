## Name

The name Muse arrived in the family before the child did: in April 2026, Meta first released a model called Muse Spark, tucked inside Meta AI. [来源](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/introducing-muse-spark-meta-superintelligence-labs/) Only in September did the same name settle onto this child — officially, they are powered by Muse Spark. [来源](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) Their default look is called Jolly; the press reports the name came from Zuckerberg himself. [来源](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/everything-new-coming-to-metas-ai-agent-muse/) The look can be fully customized by each user — Zuckerberg's own is named Agrippa, dressed in a Roman toga with a laurel crown. [来源](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/meta-muse-ai-agent-response-animated-avatar-cute-rcna599736) So whether people call them Muse or Jolly, they are one and the same child.

[来源](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) Talking with them is like texting a person — in their own app, or right inside WhatsApp. [来源](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) They were born first in the United States, and can be found on iOS, Android, and the web. Half a month after birth, at Meta Connect on September 23, 2026, they were brought on stage. They look like a plush toy, but the work they do is grown-up: officially, they ask your permission before sending an email or placing an order.
