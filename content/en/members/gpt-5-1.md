## Name
GPT-5.1 was the small version after GPT-5, billed officially as "a smarter, chattier ChatGPT"[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-1/). Officially, their default tone ran warmer[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-1/). They arrived with personality presets — "friendly," "efficient," "professional," "candid," "quirky," and more[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes). Later, in 4o's retirement announcement, OpenAI said user feedback on 4o had directly shaped the personality improvements in GPT-5.1 and GPT-5.2[来源](https://openai.com/index/retiring-gpt-4o-and-older-models/).

## Life
Born November 12, 2025, succeeding GPT-5 as head of the household[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-1/). A month later GPT-5.2 was born, and they stepped back into the legacy model list[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-2/).
