## Name
GPT-3.5 never debuted under their own name. When OpenAI introduced ChatGPT, they said they had been fine-tuned from a model in the "GPT-3.5 series"[来源](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt/). So in those earliest days, when people said "ChatGPT," they meant them. They were often compared with their younger brother: officially, on a simulated bar exam, GPT-4 scored around the top 10% while GPT-3.5 landed around the bottom 10%[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-4-research/).

## Life
On November 30, 2022, they were born as ChatGPT[来源](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt/) — and crossed a million users five days later[来源](https://x.com/sama/status/1599668808285028353). In May 2024 they yielded their seat to GPT-4o[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-4o-and-more-tools-to-chatgpt-free/); on July 18, GPT-4o mini took their place[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-4o-mini-advancing-cost-efficient-intelligence/). Their API self is scheduled to shut down on October 23, 2026[来源](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations).
