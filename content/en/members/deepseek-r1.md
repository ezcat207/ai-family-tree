## Name

DeepSeek is the name of the Hangzhou company, 杭州深度求索人工智能基础技术研究有限公司[来源](https://www.deepseek.com/news/). The family keeps no official explanation for the "R" in her name; her technical report's title speaks of "reasoning capability"[来源](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/blob/main/DeepSeek_R1.pdf). Among Chinese users she is more often called "深度思考" (Deep Thinking) — the button in the App that summons her[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250120). Two months before she was born, an elder brother arrived first as a trial: R1-Lite-Preview[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news1120).

## Life

In November 2024, her elder brother R1-Lite-Preview first tried laying his thinking process bare before people's eyes, live on the web[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news1120). On January 15, 2025, the DeepSeek App officially launched[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news250115). Five days later, on January 20, R1 was born: live on the site and App that very day, weights published under the MIT license — and the family openly said others were welcome to "distill" new models from her answers[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250120).

A week later, on January 27, the DeepSeek App topped the US App Store free chart, and on the same day Nvidia lost nearly $600 billion in market value[来源](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/tech-stocks-react-chinas-deepseek-sparks-us-worries-ai-race-rcna189394). That Spring Festival, people lined up to talk with her, and "Deep Thinking" mode kept flashing "server busy"[来源](https://www.pingwest.com/a/302288).

On May 28 she had one update; the officials said hallucinations were fewer[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250528). On August 21, V3.1 was born — the "Deep Thinking" button stayed, but pressing it now summoned V3.1[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250821). Less than a month later, on September 17, the paper describing her appeared in Nature[来源](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09422-z) — and graced that issue's cover[来源](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3471061555959170).

## Temperament

What people remember first is "think before speaking": before answering, she would spread her thinking process open for all to see[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250120). Second is her prose. The writer Li Dan summed up DeepSeek's writing as "techno-lyric prose poetry"[来源](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3993436.html). Her brother R1-Zero, published the same day, once wrote mid-training — "Wait, wait. Wait. That's an aha moment I can flag here." — and the researchers kept it verbatim in the technical report[来源](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/blob/main/DeepSeek_R1.pdf).

## Death

On August 21, 2025, DeepSeek-V3.1 was released; the official App and web chat upgraded in sync. The "Deep Thinking" button was kept — now answered by V3.1's thinking mode[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250821). That same day, the API name that had been hers, deepseek-reasoner, was pointed at V3.1 too[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250821). The announcement mentioned her only in passing, as a comparison: V3.1's thinking mode could "give answers in less time"[来源](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/zh-cn/news/news250821).

Her weights remain public in the official repo to this day — anyone may invite her back onto their own computer[来源](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1). Posthumous name candidates: 思、悟、明、念、启.
