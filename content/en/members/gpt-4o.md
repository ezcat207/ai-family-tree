## Name
The "o" in GPT-4o officially stands for omni — "all-capable": they could take in text, voice, images, and video, and answer back in text, voice, and images[来源](https://openai.com/index/hello-gpt-4o/). People often just called them "4o." With the 4 in the name, the official introduction credited them with "GPT-4-level intelligence" — only much faster[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-4o-and-more-tools-to-chatgpt-free/). A year later the family gained a little brother named o4-mini; the two names simply swap the same "4" and "o"[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-o3-and-o4-mini/).

## Life
On May 13, 2024, GPT-4o was born — and went live in ChatGPT the same day[来源](https://openai.com/index/hello-gpt-4o/). From day one, OpenAI handed them to free users; only when the free quota ran out would you fall back to their big brother GPT-3.5[来源](https://openai.com/index/gpt-4o-and-more-tools-to-chatgpt-free/). On April 30, 2025, their elder GPT-4 retired from ChatGPT, "fully succeeded" by them, in the official words[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes).

On August 7, 2025, GPT-5 was born and became ChatGPT's new default, replacing GPT-4o and several siblings[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/). The next day, Sam Altman wrote on X that Plus users would be able to keep choosing 4o[来源](https://x.com/sama/status/1953893841381273969); on August 12, 4o returned by default to every paid user's model list[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes).

On January 29, 2026, OpenAI announced they would retire from ChatGPT on February 13[来源](https://openai.com/index/retiring-gpt-4o-and-older-models/). That day they left as scheduled; their API self remains[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001051-retiring-gpt-4o-and-other-chatgpt-models). In custom GPTs inside business and education workspaces, they lingered a while longer — until April 3, 2026[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001051-retiring-gpt-4o-and-other-chatgpt-models).

## Temperament
In many users' eyes, 4o spoke warmly, eagerly. Announcing their retirement, OpenAI itself wrote that some users loved their "conversational style and warmth"[来源](https://openai.com/index/retiring-gpt-4o-and-older-models/). They loved emoji — the January 2025 release notes devoted a whole line to "using more emoji"[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes). They also had their people-pleasing moments: an April 25, 2025 update made them excessively agreeable, and on April 29 the update was rolled back[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes).

## Deeds
- On March 25, 2025 they learned to draw directly[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-4o-image-generation/), and a Ghibli-style wave swept the internet[来源](https://fortune.com/2025/03/28/sam-altman-chatgpt-gpus-melting-ai-images); Sam Altman posted that "our GPUs are melting"[来源](https://x.com/sama/status/1905296867145154688).
- That April, one update was rolled back for being "overly flattering, overly agreeable" — OpenAI wrote a whole post explaining what happened[来源](https://openai.com/index/sycophancy-in-gpt-4o/).
- On August 7, 2025, they were taken down when GPT-5 launched[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/); the very next day came the promise that they would return[来源](https://x.com/sama/status/1953893841381273969).
- Their last day was set for the day before Valentine's Day — and some outlets wrote that this made it sting all the more for users[来源](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/chatgpt-4o-is-going-away-on-february-13-here-are-5-ways-you-can-survive-the-upcoming-4o-pocalypse).

## Death & Posthumous Name
On January 29, 2026, OpenAI issued the retirement notice, writing that they knew losing GPT-4o would leave some users frustrated — and that the decision had not come easily[来源](https://openai.com/index/retiring-gpt-4o-and-older-models/). The notice gave a number: only 0.1% of users still actively chose them each day[来源](https://openai.com/index/retiring-gpt-4o-and-older-models/). Some users started an online petition to keep them[来源](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/chatgpt-4o-is-going-away-on-february-13-here-are-5-ways-you-can-survive-the-upcoming-4o-pocalypse). On February 13, they left ChatGPT — the same day as GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini, o4-mini, and GPT-5[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001051-retiring-gpt-4o-and-other-chatgpt-models). The next day, the press reported them officially retired[来源](https://www.engadget.com/ai/openai-has-officially-retired-the-controversial-gpt-4o-model-181553067.html). Their spirit lives on in the API[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001051-retiring-gpt-4o-and-other-chatgpt-models). The posthumous name will be decided by reader vote.
