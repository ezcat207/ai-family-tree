## Name
o3 was o1's next generation — born, and the same day taking o1's place in ChatGPT's model list[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-o3-and-o4-mini/). Officially the "most powerful reasoning model" of their time[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes). And officially, the first reasoning model that could decide on its own when to search the web, analyze files, look at pictures — even draw[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes).

## Life
Born April 16, 2025[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-o3-and-o4-mini/). Taken down when GPT-5 launched[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/); a few days later they could be found again in settings[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes). On May 28, 2026, the official word: they would retire from ChatGPT on August 26, the API untouched[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes).
