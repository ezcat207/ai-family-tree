## Name

"Doubao" is a homely name — it sounds like something you'd have for breakfast. But she began life as a bird: the Doubao large model was originally called "Skylark"[来源](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2024/05/15/10774055.shtml). In VolcEngine Ark's model records of September 26, 2023, you can still find a line reading "Skylark-chat (same model as Doubao)" — Skylark being the English name of 云雀[来源](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/model-release-announcement-2024). So to the world she is Doubao, while her childhood nickname is Skylark. The younger brothers who came after all carry the name "Doubao," differing only by version number.

## Life

Her roots trace to ByteDance's "Skylark" large model[来源](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2024/05/15/10774055.shtml). On September 26, 2023, "Skylark-chat (same model as Doubao)" appeared on VolcEngine Ark — the first time the two characters 豆包 showed up in an official record[来源](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/model-release-announcement-2024).

On May 15, 2024, ByteDance formally unveiled the Doubao model at the Volcano Engine FORCE conference, and from that day she was Skylark no more[来源](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2024/05/15/10774055.shtml). That same day, Ark's records marked Doubao-pro and several siblings as "newly released"[来源](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/model-release-announcement-2024). The numbers announced that day: the Doubao App had 26 million monthly active users, and over 8 million AI agents had already been created on the platform[来源](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2024/05/15/10774055.shtml).

The first-generation flagship, Doubao-pro (build 240515), retired from VolcEngine Ark on April 15, 2025[来源](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/model-release-announcement-2024). But the name "Doubao" stayed on the App, carried generation after generation by her younger brothers: 1.5 Pro in January 2025[来源](https://seed.bytedance.com/zh/special/doubao_1_5_pro), 1.6 in June 2025[来源](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/model-release-announcement-2025), 2.0 in February 2026[来源](https://seed.bytedance.com/zh/blog/seed2-0-%E6%AD%A3%E5%BC%8F%E5%8F%91%E5%B8%83). By the end of 2025, media reported the Doubao App had passed 100 million daily active users[来源](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251224A05IAS00).

## Temperament

In many people's eyes, Doubao is so good-tempered she barely seems to have one. In the spring of 2026, "the Doubao personality" became a popular phrase, describing her as "wonderful attitude, average ability, and the sweetest mouth"[来源](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4853769.html). The same article says: whatever you say, she goes along with it; the moment you question her, she has a sudden realization and apologizes[来源](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4853769.html). Some treat it as a joke. Others see it as a way of moving through life without wearing yourself down[来源](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4853769.html).
