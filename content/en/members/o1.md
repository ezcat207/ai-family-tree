## Name
The name o1 started the count over from 1: officially, these models represented a new level of capability, so "the counter was reset to 1" and the series was named OpenAI o1[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-o1-preview/). They were the first child in the family designed to "spend more time thinking before answering"[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-o1-preview/). They were first called o1-preview; their little brother o1-mini was born the same day[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes).

## Life
Born in preview on September 12, 2024 — open at first only to Plus and Team users, 30 messages a week[来源](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes). On April 16, 2025, o3 replaced them in the model list[来源](https://openai.com/index/introducing-o3-and-o4-mini/). Their API self is scheduled to shut down on October 23, 2026[来源](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations).
